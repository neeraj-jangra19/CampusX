Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026
This policy explains what information Campus X collects, who can see it and how it is protected. By creating an account you agree to it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email or college ID, and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Verification details: college name, student ID, course, year and an image or PDF of your college ID.
  • Profile details: nickname, age, gender, course, year, height, hometown, living status, interests, bio, answers to icebreaker questions and three photos.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, reports and the messages you send to your matches.

2. Who Can See Your Information

  • Your college ID document is visible only to Campus X administrators for verification.
  • Your profile (nickname, age, photos, interests, bio, answers and the basic details shown on your card) is visible to other verified students.
  • Your email or college ID, password and religion are never shown to other students.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the student you are chatting with. Chat opens only after both students like each other.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify that every member is a real student.
  • To show you relevant profiles, ordered by shared interests, course and year.
  • To let matched students message each other and to review reports.
  • We do not sell your information.

4. Security

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Uploaded files are checked for type and size, and ID documents are served only to administrators.
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies.
  • No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

5. Your Choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time from the dashboard.
  • You can report any profile, and you will no longer see it.
  • To delete your account or data, contact your Campus X administrator.
